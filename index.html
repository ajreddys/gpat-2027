About the exam

Important dates

The Graduate Pharmacy Aptitude Test (GPAT) is a national computer-based test for entry to M.Pharm courses. NBEMS conducts it, declares the result and hands it to the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). Counselling and admissions are run by the authorities concerned, not NBEMS.

The GPAT 2027 bulletin is awaited from NBEMS. Until it's out, the exam details on this page follow the GPAT 2026 information bulletin, and the 2026 dates are shown only for reference.

Percentile, rank and qualifying, explained

  • 1Rank is your overall merit position among everyone who appeared in that year's GPAT. NBEMS does not prepare category-wise or state-wise merit lists.
  • 2To qualify, you need at least the 50th percentile (General / EWS) or the 45th percentile (OBC / SC / ST, and PwBD of any category), and a place within the top 10% of all candidates who appeared, in merit order.
  • 3That top 10% is shared out by category:
40.5%UR
27%OBC
15%SC
10%EWS
7.5%ST

Each category keeps 5% for PwBD candidates (40% or more disability). Candidates below the last qualified rank but on the same score or percentile are declared not qualified.

When scores tie

  1. Fewer negative (wrong) responses ranks higher
  2. Then the older candidate
  3. Then higher Class XII marks
  4. Then higher Class X marks (CGPA × 9.5)
GPAT 2026 rules · 2027 awaited

Who can apply

  • Citizen of India
  • B.Pharm (4 years after 10+2, including lateral entry) or equivalent from a recognised university
  • Final-year (4th year) B.Pharm students can appear, if the result comes before M.Pharm admissions begin
  • Pre-final (3rd year) students can also appear, for M.Pharm admission a year later

B.E. / B.Tech. (Pharmaceutical and Fine Chemical Technology) and equivalent are not eligible. Eligibility is provisional and is checked at counselling.

The paper and the result

  • Pharmacology & allied28

Multiple choice with 4 options, in English only. +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong one (25% negative marking), 0 if unattempted. The paper runs in time-bound sections: with 5 sections, each has 25 questions and 36 minutes, and you can't go back to a section once its time ends.

  1. CBT

    125 MCQs, 180 minutes, one computer-based paper.

  2. Result

    Raw marks under the final answer key give your score and all-India rank.

  3. Qualify

    50th percentile (45th for OBC, SC, ST, PwBD) and the top 10% of candidates.

  4. Use the score

    For M.Pharm and PhD admission at PCI-approved institutions. Valid 3 years.

Syllabus

The syllabus covers the subjects and knowledge areas of "The Bachelor of Pharmacy (B. Pharm.) Course Regulations, 2024". NBEMS gives the blueprint below. A topic-wise list is awaited from NBEMS. Under each section is what our batch covers there; the grouping is ours.

Pharmaceutical Chemistry & allied 38 Qs · 152

Covered in our batch:

  • Pharmaceutical Inorganic and Organic Chemistry
  • Medicinal Chemistry; drug design and CADD

  • Pharmaceutical Analysis; Instrumental Methods; Advanced Instrumentation
Pharmaceutics & allied 38 Qs · 152

Covered in our batch:

  • Pharmaceutics; Physical Pharmaceutics
  • Pharmaceutical Engineering; Industrial Pharmacy

  • Novel Drug Delivery Systems; Cosmetic Science; Quality Assurance
